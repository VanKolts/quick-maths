How to use

  1. Start (or press Enter) gives you five minutes; Endless runs until you press Stop. Use Focus to practise only the operations you pick.
  2. Type your answer and press Enter. Most answers are whole numbers; when a question asks you to differentiate, integrate, simplify, expand or factorise, type an expression instead — ^ for a power, r for √, / for a fraction, brackets to group, e.g. 3x^2 - 4x^(1/2) or 6r2 for 6√2. A preview shows how your answer is being read. For an integral, “+ c” is optional.
  3. Hint (?) shows the general method or formula for that kind of question. A hinted answer does not count as right first time.
  4. Skip (Esc) sets a question aside. Get one wrong and it comes back a few questions later, marked; get it wrong again and it is set aside. Everything set aside comes back, untimed, at the end.
  5. Each operation has its own level, which rises and falls with your accuracy and speed — see the table above.
