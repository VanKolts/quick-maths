How to use

  1. Start (or press Enter) runs for the length set under Focus — five minutes unless you change it, or Endless, which runs until you press Stop. Focus also picks the operations and levels.
  2. Type your answer and press Enter. Most answers are whole numbers; when a question asks you to differentiate, integrate, simplify, expand or factorise, type an expression instead. / turns what you just typed into the top of a fraction and puts you in the bottom; / on its own starts an empty fraction. ^ opens a power, r a square root. You stay in a box until you press Tab or → (on a phone, the ⇥ button) — so x^1/2 then Tab is x to the half. Each press steps out of one box, so a fraction inside a power takes two. A dashed box shows where the next part goes; Backspace on an empty box removes it. For an integral, “+ c” is optional.
  3. Hint (?) shows the general method or formula for that kind of question. A hinted answer does not count as right first time.
  4. Skip (Esc) when you cannot start a question: it comes back a few questions later with a worked start — the first steps done with its own numbers. Get one wrong and it also comes back later, marked. Wrong again, or skipped again, and it is set aside: everything set aside comes back, untimed, at the end.
  5. Each operation has its own level, which rises and falls with your accuracy and speed — see below.

Reading the table

Level
1–3, separately for each operation. Questions come from level 1 up to the highest level you have reached — half at your current level, the rest from the levels below (or above, if you have dropped back) — so the basics keep coming up. Only questions at your current level move it. After 5 answers at a level it rises if at least 80% of your recent answers were right and your median time is under 14 s (23 s for surds, polynomials and calculus); it drops if fewer than half were right. Every operation in your focus comes up at least once in every 30 questions.
Seen
Questions answered (first attempts; skips count).
Accuracy
Share right first time, without a hint, over everything you have answered.
Median
Your middle time over your last 40 questions answered right first time — half were faster, half slower. Wrong answers, skips and second tries add no time. Greyed when there are fewer than 3 answers, and only those with 3 or more are ranked for “slowest”.
